💡 how to write great reviews

📝 be honest but kind

share ur true thoughts!!! whether they be negative, positive, or just neutral, feel free to express all your opinions on each book. it is okay to be critical, but please refrain from making personal attacks.

🏷️ tag thoughtfully

tags help others find books they'll enjoy! always start with fiction or nonfiction, then add genre tags (romance, mystery, fantasy), mood tags (cozy, dark, heartwarming), or content tags (plot-twist, slow-burn)

fiction examples: fiction, fantasy, ya, enemies-to-lovers, found-family, page-turner

nonfiction examples: nonfiction, memoir, self-help, history, biography, educational

✨ what to include

  • • what u loved (or didn't love) about it
  • • the vibes & pacing
  • • characters & how they made u feel
  • • who would enjoy this book
  • • anything unique or special about it
